# Madre Pulse — What It Does

Madre Pulse is a task and project management tool for teams. It helps managers assign work, track progress, and see how the team is doing — and helps team members know what to work on and keep it updated.

## What's available so far

1. **Accounts & sign-in** — create your company workspace (organization name, your name, email, password) and you're signed in as its admin. Sign out and back in any time; your session stays signed in across page reloads and browser restarts.
2. **Team & client setup** — admins add teammates (name, email, role — a temporary password is shown once to share with them; there's no invite email yet), manage roles, and enable/disable access. Admins and managers add the clients you do work for and the projects you run, optionally tagged to a client; everyone on the team can see the team, client, and project lists.
3. **Tasks** — anyone can create a task, set its priority and due date, and optionally link it to a project and assign it to one or more teammates who all work on it together. Filter the list by status or assignee, and change a task's status inline. You can edit a task if you created it, you're one of its assignees, or you're an admin/manager; only admins and managers can delete a task.
4. **Board view** — on the Tasks page, switch to "Board" to see tasks as cards in To Do / In Progress / Done columns. Drag a card to a different column to change its status — the same edit permissions apply, so a card you can't edit won't budge.
5. **Calendar view** — switch to "Calendar" on the Tasks page for a month grid showing tasks on their due date, color-coded by priority. Navigate months with the arrows or jump back to today. Tasks without a due date don't show up here — check List or Board for those.
6. **Sub-tasks & dependencies** — click a task's title (from the List view) to open its detail page. Break it into a checklist of subtasks with a progress count, and hand each one to a specific teammate (from among the task's own assignees) so it's clear who's doing which piece of a shared task. Mark other tasks it's blocked by — the app won't let you create a circular chain of blockers.
7. **Comments, attachments & activity** — on a task's detail page, anyone on the team can discuss it, attach files, and add or remove assignees, regardless of who's currently on the task. Every status, priority, assignee, and due-date change is logged automatically and shown alongside the comments in one timeline. You can delete your own comments and files any time; admins and managers can moderate anyone's.
8. **Time tracking** — on a task's detail page, log hours spent (in decimal, e.g. 1.5) with an optional note and date — anyone on the team can log time on any task. The total logged so far is shown at a glance. You can edit or remove your own entries any time; admins and managers can correct anyone's.
9. **Notifications** — a bell icon shows updates the moment they happen — no need to refresh the page — when you're assigned a task, someone comments on a task you're involved in, or a task you're assigned is due soon. Notifications you haven't opened yet are also emailed to you (once real email delivery is configured — until then they're just logged). Click a notification to jump to the task, and mark them read individually or all at once.
10. **Reopening completed work** — if a task marked Done needs more work, anyone who created it, is assigned to it, or is an admin/manager can request it be reopened. An admin or manager approves or rejects the request; approving moves the task back to To Do and notifies whoever asked.
11. **Manager dashboard** — admins and managers get an at-a-glance view of the team: tasks completed in the last 7 days, on-time completion rate, overdue tasks, and a workload breakdown per teammate.
12. **Reports** — admins and managers can pull a productivity table (tasks completed, on-time rate, time logged per person), optionally filtered to a date range, and download it as a CSV. The page also prints cleanly to PDF straight from the browser's print dialog.
13. **Reporting a blocker** — if something's stopping you from moving a task forward, report it right from the task's detail page with a short reason. Every admin and manager is notified immediately, and once it's sorted out, they mark it resolved — which notifies you back. No need to formally link it to another task; it's just a way to flag "I'm stuck" and have it actually reach the people who can help.
14. **Organization settings** — admins can rename the workspace from a new Settings page, and see it at a glance: how many active members you have and when the workspace was created. Every workspace's data is completely private to it — nobody outside your organization can ever see or touch it, and this has been true since day one, not something bolted on later.

## What's coming, in order

Nothing further is planned for this phase — Phase 1 is feature-complete. Future phases may add things like auto time tracking, recurring tasks, integrations (Slack/GitHub/Google Calendar), dashboard customization, SSO, and billing.
